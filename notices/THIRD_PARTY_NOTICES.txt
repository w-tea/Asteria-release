Asteria V1.0.4 Third-Party Notices

Asteria downloads and uses third-party runtimes, packages, custom nodes, and
model assets. The authoritative pinned identities, provider URLs, provenance
links, and license URLs are recorded in release-manifest.yaml,
runtime-dependencies.yaml, and model-dependencies.yaml.

Optional model downloads remain subject to their provider terms. The Anima
starter pack requires explicit acceptance of its non-commercial license before
download. Asteria does not grant additional rights to third-party components or
model files.
